# Sample size derivation notes

This project used publicly available GWAS summary statistics.

## Overall endometriosis excluding 23andMe

The publicly released European-ancestry overall endometriosis summary statistics used in this study excluded the 23andMe component.

Case and control counts were not directly reported for this 23andMe-excluded public release. Approximate counts were derived by subtracting the reported 23andMe contribution from the full European combined endometriosis meta-analysis reported by Koller et al. (2026).

- Full European combined endometriosis meta-analysis: 99,407 cases and 1,093,534 controls
- Reported 23andMe contribution: 37,183 cases and 251,258 controls
- Approximate 23andMe-excluded overall endometriosis sample size: 62,224 cases and 842,276 controls

The arithmetic used was:

- Cases: 99,407 − 37,183 = 62,224
- Controls: 1,093,534 − 251,258 = 842,276

These counts should therefore be described as approximate and derived, rather than as directly reported file-level sample sizes.

## Other endometriosis-spectrum GWAS datasets

- Adenomyosis: 8,753 cases and 415,718 controls
- Endometriosis without adenomyosis: 6,627 cases and 283,316 controls

## Endometrial cancer GWAS datasets

- All-histology endometrial cancer: 12,906 cases and 108,979 controls; total N = 121,885
- Endometrioid endometrial cancer: 8,758 cases and 46,126 controls; total N = 54,884
